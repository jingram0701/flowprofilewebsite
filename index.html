What we do

Metal roofing, fascia, gutters and cladding are our specialty. We also take on small general plumbing jobs.

Metal roofing

New metal roofs and re-roofs on homes, extensions and new builds, with the detail work that stops leaks.

  • Re-roofs and new roofs in steel
  • Leak detection and repairs
  • Flashings, capping and valleys
  • Skylights and roof penetrations
  • Insulation and sarking

Fascia and gutters

Fascia, gutters and downpipes that are colour matched, sized right and carry the water away from the building.

  • Fascia replacement
  • New gutters and downpipes
  • Rainheads and box gutters
  • Gutter repairs and maintenance

Cladding

External wall cladding that is weather-tight and finished cleanly at every corner, window and join.

  • Steel wall cladding, standing seam and profiled
  • Non-combustible cladding replacement
  • Soffits and eaves linings
  • Garages, sheds and outbuildings

Small plumbing jobs

General plumbing by a licensed plumber, for the smaller jobs around the house.

  • Leak and burst repairs
  • Stormwater and drainage
  • Maintenance and compliance checks

How a job runs

Most jobs follow the same four steps, whether it is a gutter repair or a full re-roof.

  1. Get in touch

    Call, or send a few details through the form below. A photo of the problem helps.

  2. Site visit and fixed quote

    We come out, measure, check what is underneath, and send a written quote with a fixed price and a start date.

  3. The work

    We turn up when we say we will, keep the site safe and tidy, and tell you if anything changes before it costs you.

  4. Handover

    We walk you through what was done, hand over any compliance certificates, and clean up before we leave.
