Roof leaking? What to do before the plumber arrives

By Brenton J Fitzgerald, Licensed Plumber · 15 October 2026 · 2 min read

A roof leak always seems to start at the worst time. Here is what to do to limit the damage while you wait for help.

1. Stay safe

Do not climb onto a wet roof. If water is near light fittings or power points, turn off the power to that area at the switchboard.

2. Catch the water

Put buckets or containers under drips and move furniture and rugs out of the way. If a ceiling is bulging with water, keep clear of it.

3. Take photos

Photograph the leak inside, any stains, and the roof and gutters from the ground. This helps your roof plumber, and your insurer if you need to make a claim.

4. Check the gutters from the ground

Many leaks are not the roof at all. If gutters are overflowing, water can run back under the roof edge and into the ceiling. Look for water spilling over the gutter edge during rain.

5. Call a licensed roof plumber

Tell them where the water is showing, when it started, and whether it only leaks in heavy rain or wind. That helps narrow down the cause before they arrive.

Common causes

  • Failed or lifted flashings around chimneys, vents and walls
  • Loose or rusted screws
  • Blocked or undersized gutters
  • Rust holes in old sheets
  • Cracked seals around skylights
