Rotten fascia: what it is, why it matters and when to replace it

By Brenton J Fitzgerald, Licensed Plumber · 15 October 2026 · 2 min read

The fascia is the board that runs along the edge of your roof, just under the gutters. Most people never notice it until it starts to fail.

What fascia does

Fascia closes off the ends of the roof rafters, keeps weather and pests out of the roof space, and gives your gutters something solid to fix to. If it fails, the gutters go with it.

How to spot rotten fascia

  • Paint peeling or bubbling along the roof edge
  • Soft spots when you press the timber
  • Gutters sagging or pulling away
  • Dark staining or mould on the board
  • Birds, possums or insects getting into the roof

Why it rots

Nearly always, it is water. Overflowing gutters soak the back of the fascia, and timber that stays wet will rot. Fixing the fascia without fixing the gutters just starts the clock again.

Replace with timber or steel?

Steel fascia does not rot, does not need painting and can be matched to your gutters and roof. Timber can be replaced like for like if you prefer the look, but it needs regular painting to last.

Do it with the gutters

Because the gutters are fixed to the fascia, it often makes sense to replace both at once. You get a straight, solid edge and gutters set at the right fall from day one.
