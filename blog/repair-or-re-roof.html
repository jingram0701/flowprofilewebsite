Repair or re-roof? Signs your metal roof is due for replacement

By Brenton J Fitzgerald, Licensed Plumber · 15 October 2026 · 2 min read

Most metal roofs give plenty of warning before they fail. The trick is knowing which signs mean a quick repair and which mean it is time to start budgeting for a new roof.

Signs a repair will do

  • One leak in one spot. A single leak is usually a failed flashing, a lifted screw or a cracked seal around a vent. These are fixable.
  • Loose or missing screws. Fixings work loose over time. Replacing them is quick and cheap.
  • Damaged ridge capping. Capping can be refixed or replaced without touching the sheets.

Signs it is time to re-roof

  • Rust through the sheets. Surface marks can be treated. Rust that has eaten through the steel cannot.
  • Leaks in several places. If you are chasing a new leak every winter, the roof is telling you something.
  • Sagging or uneven sheets. This can point to problems with the battens or frame underneath.
  • Old tiles being swapped out. If you are replacing a tiled roof anyway, steel is lighter and quicker to install.

What a re-roof involves

The old roofing comes off, the battens are checked and replaced where needed, sarking and insulation go down, then the new sheets, flashings and capping go on. A typical house is usually done over a few days, weather permitting, with the house kept watertight each night.

What to ask your roofer

  • Is this a repair that will last, or a patch?
  • What is underneath the sheets, and is it sound?
  • Are gutters and fascia included, or priced separately?
  • Is the quote a fixed price?
