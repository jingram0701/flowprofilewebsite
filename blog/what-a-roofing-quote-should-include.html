What a fixed-price roofing quote should include

By Brenton J Fitzgerald, Licensed Plumber · 15 October 2026 · 2 min read

Two quotes for the same roof can look very different. Here is what a clear, fixed-price quote should cover, so you can compare like with like.

The checklist

  • Scope of work. Exactly what is being replaced or repaired, and what is not.
  • Materials. The type, profile and colour of steel, plus sarking and insulation if included.
  • Gutters, fascia and downpipes. Included, or priced separately?
  • Removal and disposal. Who takes away the old roofing and rubbish.
  • Fixed price. A total, not an estimate, with any allowances clearly listed.
  • Start date and timeframe. When work starts and roughly how long it takes.
  • Licence and insurance. The plumber's licence number and confirmation of insurance.
  • Compliance. Whether a compliance certificate is provided at handover.
  • Warranty. Workmanship warranty, plus manufacturer warranties on materials.

Red flags

  • A price with no breakdown of what is included
  • Pressure to sign on the spot
  • A large deposit before any materials are ordered
  • No licence number on the quote

How we quote

We visit, measure, check what is underneath and send a written quote with a fixed price and a start date. If anything changes once work starts, we tell you before it costs you.
