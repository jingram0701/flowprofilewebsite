Why steel cladding

Steel is durable, low maintenance and non-combustible. It gives a clean, modern look and ties in with a metal roof, fascia and gutters for a finish that matches from top to bottom.

The details that matter

Good cladding is mostly about the edges: corners, window and door surrounds, and where the walls meet the roof. That is where water gets in if the flashing is rushed, and where a job looks sharp or sloppy. We take our time on all of them.

Choosing a profile

Standing seam gives long, clean vertical lines. Profiled sheeting is a more traditional, cost-effective option. Our guide Standing seam vs profiled steel cladding compares the two.

Common questions

Can you replace existing cladding?

Yes, including replacing older cladding with a non-combustible steel system.

Do you do sheds and garages?

Yes. Steel cladding suits garages, sheds and outbuildings well.

Can cladding match my roof?

Yes. Using the same steel colours across roof, fascia, gutters and walls gives a consistent finish.
