Why metal

A steel roof is light, quick to install and, when it is fixed and flashed properly, keeps water out for decades. It suits Melbourne weather, from long dry summers to heavy winter rain, and it pairs neatly with steel gutters, fascia and cladding.

Repair or replace?

Not every leak needs a new roof. We check the sheets, fixings, flashings and gutters first, then tell you straight whether a repair will last or a re-roof is the better spend. Read more in Repair or re-roof? Signs your metal roof is due for replacement.

How a roofing job runs

  1. You call or send a few details and photos.
  2. We visit, measure and check what is underneath.
  3. You get a written, fixed-price quote with a start date.
  4. We do the work, keep the site tidy and hand over any compliance certificates.

Common questions

Do you work on tiled roofs?

We specialise in metal roofing. If your tiled roof is at the end of its life, we can replace it with a new steel roof.

Can you fix a leak without replacing the roof?

Often, yes. Many leaks come from failed flashings, lifted fixings or blocked gutters, which can be repaired on their own.

Is roof plumbing work certified?

Yes. Brent is a licensed plumber and provides compliance certificates for work that requires one.
